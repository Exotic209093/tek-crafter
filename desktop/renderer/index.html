INVENTORYCRAFTING

Crafts from your own inventory (Tab → Crafting). For each item: select it, type the pull amount and press the pull button (top-left of your inventory), then press A. Runs as its own job and never travels, because fast travel drops your items.

min
CALIBRATEBEDS

Each bed remembers where every bench is from its spawn spot. Recalibrate a bed if you move it or its benches. Takes about 5 min; hands off the mouse while it runs.

SETTINGSHOTKEYS

Hotkeys work while ARK is focused. Click a key, then press the new one (F-keys, numpad or mouse side buttons). Esc cancels.
